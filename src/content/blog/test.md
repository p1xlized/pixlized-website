## Tricky interview questions and how to solve them.
The interview starts the second you join the call. Not when the first real question gets asked.

### Hello, How are you?
<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Never be honest here!</mark> -> Instead say
<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">I'm doing great, How about you.</mark> 
They start screening you at the begining, so if you say something negative they will take note and think that when they day is rough you won't be productive.

*You're not being fake, you're showing the recruter that you can regulate your energy when it matters.*

----

### Why do you want to work here?

*I went to your website and I really love your mission and values.*
    
   It tells them nothing about you. It tells them that you spent five minutes on our about page. 
   -> <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">So what you want to do is flip the question entirely.</mark>Stop making it about <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">what you want from them</mark> and make it about <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">what you're going to do for them.</mark>
   
*I've outgrown my current role and I'm looking for a new challenge. And based on what I've seen about this team's goals, I know I can help you solve and then insert the problem.* 
#### It tells them that:
1. You're there to contribute
2. You already know what you will be doing for them.
